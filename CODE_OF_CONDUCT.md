# Research community conduct

Be precise about evidence and generous toward people. Welcome contributors across disciplines, countries, languages and career stages. Critique methods and claims, not identities. Do not harass, discriminate, expose personal information, impersonate authors or fabricate records.

Maintainers may request revisions or remove contributions that are irrelevant, abusive, plagiarized or knowingly misleading. Report repository-content concerns through a minimal, factual issue without sharing sensitive personal details. For personal safety or harassment reports, use GitHub's private reporting channels rather than posting identifying information publicly.
